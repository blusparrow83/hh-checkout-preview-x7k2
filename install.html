HH Checkout v6 Viewer

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: HH Checkout v6
  2. Sign in to the store as admin, add something to the cart, and open the Checkout page.
  3. Click the bookmark. The new checkout appears in your browser only. Customers never see it, and reloading the page brings the old one back.

No order is placed by viewing. Use the real Place Order button only when you mean to.
